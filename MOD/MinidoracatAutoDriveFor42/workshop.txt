description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Requires the base mod: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]Minidoracat MiniMap for B42[/url]

description=[/list]
description=Keep every Minidoracat MiniMap series mod up to date and restart the game after updating.
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Get a [b]GPS Navigator[/b] and an [b]Autopilot Module[/b]; see the full guide for loot spots and parts
description=[*] Standing beside the vehicle, install them via [b]right-click or Vehicle Mechanics[/b]
description=[*] Open the world map and pick a destination to plan a route
description=[*] Sit in the driver's seat and press [b]Engage Autodrive[/b] on the panel above the dashboard
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Vehicle devices[/b]: GPS and Autopilot are real vehicle parts; loot, craft or learn the recipes
description=[*] [b]Multi-target trips[/b]: up to 16 targets, with stopovers that wait for Continue autodrive
description=[*] [b]Driver HUD and voice[/b]: status, speed, time left and power/fuel at a glance, plus Chinese/English/Japanese voice prompts in three voices, plus a short, casual friend style
description=[*] [b]Speed gears[/b]: regular gears drive comfortably; MAX takes corners and dodges more briskly
description=[*] [b]Take over anytime[/b]: steering, throttle or braking hands control back and keeps the trip
description=[*] [b]Smart driving[/b]: keeps right, steers around parked vehicles and obstacles, avoids zombies, corpses and animals, and slows for bends and traffic
description=[*] [b]Blocked-road recovery and rerouting[/b]: reverses out when stuck; on a fully blocked road it goes around off-road or asks navigation for an alternative route
description=[*] [b]Server tuning[/b]: power, fuel, crafting and loot are sandbox options; server owners can opt in to diagnostics that help improve autodrive
description=[/list]
description=🗺️ Whether a map mod supports navigation and autodrive depends on its author providing correct road data.
description=📖 [b]Full details, settings and FAQ for every feature:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3792675881/586187095760051144/]AutoDrive Guide: Features, Road Requirements & Known Issues[/url]
