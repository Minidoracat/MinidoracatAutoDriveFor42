description=[h2]✨ What is this[/h2]
description=Item-driven vehicle navigation and autodrive: plan routes with a GPS Navigator, then let the Autopilot Module drive along the road network
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Get a [b]GPS Navigator[/b] and an [b]Autopilot Module[/b] (loot, or craft with Electrical 3 / 6)
description=[*] Stand next to your vehicle, [b]right-click the vehicle[/b] → "Install Autopilot Module" (needs a screwdriver, a vehicle battery and Electrical 1). Installing the GPS is optional - it also works from your inventory with a battery
description=[*] Open the world map and pick a destination to plan a route
description=[*] Sit in the driver's seat and press [b]Engage Autodrive[/b] on the panel above the dashboard. Touch the controls at any time to take over
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]🧰 Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]GPS Navigator[/b]: loot or craft one, then pick a destination on the world map to plan a route along the road network
description=[*] [b]Autopilot Module[/b]: install it into a vehicle to drive along the planned route automatically and stop on arrival
description=[*] [b]Driver HUD[/b]: shows autodrive state, speed, cruise limit, gear, slowdown reason, power and fuel; start/stop or change gear directly. Four themes (metal, glass, family card, side wings - two panels beside the dashboard that never cover the road, each foldable), plus compact and collapsed modes; style, hide, voice and volume are one click on the HUD itself
description=[*] [b]Speed gears and Insane mode[/b]: switch between 30 / 50 / 70 km/h and Insane (vehicle max speed). For the initial release, Insane deliberately shares the stable path-following, cornering and obstacle-safety logic, so its main difference is the cruise cap; a future update will rework it into a more aggressive, genuinely wild driving style
description=[*] [b]Take over anytime[/b]: steering, throttle or braking immediately returns control to the player; autodrive resumes only after the controls stay idle
description=[*] [b]Keep right and dodge obstacles[/b]: drives on the right by default, naturally separates oncoming traffic, finds passable gaps around parked vehicles and returns to its lane
description=[*] [b]Blocked-road recovery and rerouting[/b]: tries another gap and reverses out when stuck; on a fully blocked road it waits and the HUD shows a Reroute button that asks navigation for an alternative route around the blockage (or enable "Reroute automatically when blocked" in the options); only when no route exists does it hand control back with a notice
description=[*] [b]Voice prompts[/b]: one line each for start, stop, blocked ahead, reversing out, handing back, arrival and reroute found / not found - audible to you only; follows the game language (Chinese / English / Japanese) or a chosen voice pack, with an on/off toggle and volume slider on the HUD
description=[*] [b]Risk-aware slowdown[/b]: adjusts speed for bends, heading error, zombies, corpses, moving vehicles and unloaded areas; HUD tooltips explain the active policy
description=[*] [b]Future trajectory[/b]: translucent blue for the normal route and yellow for committed dodges; toggle it and choose line width in MOD Options or the new MiniMap AutoDrive category
description=[*] [b]Item availability[/b]: both devices can be crafted or found in world loot, with separate crafting and spawn toggles for GPS and autodrive
description=[*] [b]Power and fuel costs[/b]: GPS and autodrive each have independent 0–500% power and extra-fuel settings that stack; at 100%, active GPS navigation adds 5% fuel use and autodrive adds 25%
description=[*] [b]Map mod support out of the box[/b]: the road network comes from the game's official street data, so subscribed map mods just work
description=[*] [b]Three ways to learn the recipes[/b]: read the dedicated Electronic Navigation Repair Manual; reverse-engineer without consuming the device—a GPS teaches its recipe at Electricity 3, while an Autopilot Module teaches the GPS recipe at 3 and its own at 6; or auto-learn at Electricity 6/8. Crafting still requires Electricity 3/6. The manual appears only in electronic, computer-book, library, and magazine containers whose loot has not yet generated; existing containers are not backfilled
description=[/list]
