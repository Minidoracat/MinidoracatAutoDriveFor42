description=[h2]✨ What is this[/h2]
description=Item-driven vehicle navigation and autodrive: plan routes with a GPS Navigator, then let the Autopilot Module drive along the road network
description=
description=[h2]🧰 Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]GPS Navigator[/b]: loot or craft one, then pick a destination on the world map to plan a route along the road network
description=[*] [b]Autopilot Module[/b]: install it into a vehicle to drive along the planned route automatically, stopping on arrival
description=[*] [b]Item availability[/b]: both devices can be crafted or found in world loot, with separate crafting and spawn toggles for GPS and auto-drive
description=[*] [b]Power and fuel costs[/b]: GPS and auto-drive each have independent 0–500% power and extra-fuel settings that stack; at 100%, active GPS navigation adds 5% fuel use and auto-drive adds 25%
description=[*] [b]Performance first[/b]: precomputed route speed profiles and event-driven dodging keep per-frame cost minimal
description=[*] [b]Map mod support out of the box[/b]: the road network comes from the game's official street data, so subscribed map mods just work
description=[/list]
description=
